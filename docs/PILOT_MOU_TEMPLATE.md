# Letter of Intent — FPO Carbon Pilot

> Template for Phase 0. A signed LOI from even one FPO converts the pilot
> from "plan" to "deploying" — real traction that no deck can fake. Fill in
> the brackets, print on FPO letterhead.

---

**Letter of Intent — Carbon Verification Pilot**

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

From: [FPO Name], Registered under [Act], Registration No: [___],
Address: [___] (the "FPO")

To: [CarbonX entity/maintainer name] (the "Platform")

1. **Purpose.** The FPO intends to participate in a carbon-verification
   pilot covering approximately [200–500] of its member farmers across
   [villages/mandal/district], using the Platform's measurement, reporting
   and verification (MRV) system.

2. **Pilot scope.** The pilot covers: farmer onboarding (including FPO-
   assisted onboarding), plot boundary mapping, practice declaration and
   satellite verification, credit estimation, and — subject to buyer
   participation — sale of verified credits with transparent payout
   splitting.

3. **Farmer consent & data.** Participation is voluntary per member. Each
   farmer's data is collected with explicit consent under the DPDP Act
   2023. The Platform stores only minimal identity data (phone, name,
   last-4 of Aadhaar where provided) and plot/practice information. The
   FPO will assist members in understanding the consent terms in Telugu.

4. **Economics (subject to definitive agreement).** The Platform will
   guarantee a minimum **70% of gross credit sale value to the farmer**,
   paid via UPI/bank, with **5% to the FPO where the FPO performs
   verification or onboarding work**, and 25% to the Platform. All splits
   will be visible to the farmer in the application.

5. **Verification work.** The FPO will nominate [1–2] officers as
   verification desk operators for flagged-record review and assisted
   onboarding during the pilot.

6. **Non-binding.** This letter is a statement of intent to negotiate a
   definitive pilot agreement. It does not create exclusive obligations.

For [FPO Name]:                    For the Platform:

______________________             ______________________
[Name, Designation]                [Name]

## What CarbonX commits to the FPO in exchange

- Verification desk training for nominated officers (2 hours).
- The FPO desk dashboard (onboarding workbench, flagged queue, pooled
  credits view) — already built.
- 5% share on every credit the FPO did verification work on, visible
  per-credit in the ledger.
- Season-end impact report per member (credits, earnings, NDVI trend).
